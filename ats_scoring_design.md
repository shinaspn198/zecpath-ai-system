# Day 13 – ATS Scoring Formula Design

## Scoring Parameters

| Parameter | Description | Weight |
|-----------|-------------|--------|
| Skill Match | Percentage of required skills found in the resume | 40% |
| Experience Relevance | Measures how well the candidate's experience matches the job | 25% |
| Education Alignment | Checks whether the required education is satisfied | 15% |
| Semantic Similarity | AI similarity score between resume and job description | 20% |

Total Weight = 100%
